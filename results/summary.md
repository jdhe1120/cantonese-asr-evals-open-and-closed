- **CantoCaptions v2**: rookes/cantocaptions-cantonese-asr at d9e2680 (Sep 20 2026 weights): Qwen/Qwen3-ASR-1.7B-hf + LoRA rank 128, one epoch on ~75 h of CantoCaptions audio, merged weights.
- **Qwen3-ASR-1.7B**: Qwen/Qwen3-ASR-1.7B-hf at bcd2b5b, untuned baseline.
- **Qwen3-ASR-1.7B + HK converter**: qwen3-asr-stock's transcripts converted to Hong Kong traditional (OpenCC s2t plus cantocaptions-ai's Cantonese variant table; cantoeval/convert.py). Traditional view only.
- **Qwen3-ASR-1.7B prompted for traditional**: Qwen/Qwen3-ASR-1.7B-hf at bcd2b5b (same weights as the stock row) with a system prompt asking for traditional characters; the prompt is qwen_trad.CHOSEN in cantoeval/models/qwen_trad.py, picked on a 100-clip test.
- **Qwen3-ASR-1.7B prompted for traditional + HK converter**: qwen3-asr-trad's transcripts converted to Hong Kong traditional (OpenCC s2t plus cantocaptions-ai's Cantonese variant table; cantoeval/convert.py). Traditional view only.

- **SenseVoice-Small-Yue**: ASLP-lab/WSYue-ASR at 4a74a91, sensevoice_small_yue: SenseVoiceSmall fine-tuned on WenetSpeech-Yue (ASLP@NPU, 2025). Run like the stock row: FunASR + fsmn-vad at df20e6b, language yue, ITN on.

- **Whisper-medium-Yue**: ASLP-lab/WSYue-ASR at 4a74a91, whisper_medium_yue: Whisper medium (769M) fine-tuned on WenetSpeech-Yue, WeNet a54b90b, CPU, attention decoding.
